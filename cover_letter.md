# Cover Letter

Hello! you're reading this because your job application required a cover letter. I do not do these on principle. Writing a unique cover letter per-employer is an unreasonable ask during a boom market, let alone during an fiscal crisis brought on during an economic collapse.

The number of unpaid global man hours wasted on writing a puff-piece for an organisation is unacceptable, and honestly cruel, and perhaps your organisations ethics board should review it.

It is quite disingeuous to waste your own time reading AI generated candidate slop. I don't want to disrespect your time making you read the thoughts of a clanker.

### Do you like theater?

Is it worthwhile to ask your candidates to:

 * Spend hours trying to say just the right lies to entice your HR team (or get past ATS).
 * Train candidates to lie and be disingenuous about their motivations to you.
 * Potentially reject your position entirely due to friction.
 * Generate AI slop for your human eyeballs to read to avoid the friction.

### Or would you prefer radical candour?

You have spreadsheets and CRUD forms that need work. I have rent to pay. I'm sure we can come to a mutually benefitial productive arrangement but I don't want to start this business relationship on the wrong foot by lying to your face about it. No developer is going to be excited for your open job role, thats why you have to attach a salary to it in the first place. The unemployed developer isn't enthused to help grow your bottom line or enable your goals and vision, they've got no skin in the game yet. 

So lets start off on the right foot.
